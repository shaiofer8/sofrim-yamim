## Findings

- **"Countdown to Anything" (com.jupli.countdowntoanything): 500K+ downloads, 4.5–4.6★, ~5.1K–5.28K reviews, last updated 2026-07-09 (per direct fetch; a search-engine-synthesized secondary source separately said "May 5, 2026").** Free app, explicitly advertises "No ads! ... no ads and no data sharing at all"; monetizes via Premium features offered as a one-time lifetime purchase OR a yearly subscription, with a 7-day free trial (exact price not shown on listing). [Source: Google Play via r.jina.ai text proxy, "Countdown to Anything", accessed 2026-08-17, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jupli.countdowntoanything&hl=en_US; corroborating: Google Search snippet aggregating APK/store data, accessed 2026-08-17]. Confidence: high (two independent reads agree on install bracket and rating; exact "updated" date varies by ~2 months across sources, likely reflecting different fetch times / cached snippets). Class: competitor.

- **"Time Until: Countdowns, Widget" (com.brunoschalch.timeuntil, developer "Handcrafted Apps and Games" / Bruno Schalch Garcia): 5M+ downloads (direct fetch) vs "5.8 million" per a secondary aggregator search snippet — both indicate multi-million scale. Rating 4.56–4.6★ on ~48K–49.8K reviews. Last updated ~2026-07-20/07-30.** Free with ads AND in-app purchases (listing discloses "Contains ads / In-app purchases"); premium tier reportedly includes cloud backups, ad-free experience, and exclusive themes; one user review flagged "the monthly fee is way too high," implying a subscription-style remove-ads/premium tier rather than a single one-time IAP. [Source: Google Play via r.jina.ai, accessed 2026-08-17, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brunoschalch.timeuntil&hl=en_US; corroborating aggregator-derived search snippet, accessed 2026-08-17]. Confidence: high on scale/rating, medium on exact monetization structure (subscription vs one-time not fully disclosed on listing itself). Class: competitor.

- **"Countdown Days App & Widget" (smsr.com.cw, developer "Glad Appvestor" / Appvestor ApS): 10M+ downloads — the largest install base found in this research — but the lowest rating of the surveyed set at 3.3–3.35★ on ~120K–129K reviews.** Free with ads and in-app purchases; listed "Updated on" date differs by source (2026-06-09 via direct fetch vs "November 17, 2025" via secondary aggregator). User reviews cited in search results express distrust over a permission change requiring phone-call access to run the app, cited as harming trust/rating. [Source: Google Play via r.jina.ai, accessed 2026-08-17, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=smsr.com.cw&hl=en_US; corroborating aggregator search snippet, accessed 2026-08-17]. Confidence: high on scale (10M+, two sources agree on this bracket and the ~3.3★ rating), medium on the specific permission-complaint detail (mentioned via a single secondary summary of reviews, not independently read in full). Class: competitor.

- **"Big Days - Events Countdown" (com.astrovicapp.bigDaysLite, developer Astrovic App / Vittorio Tindaro Sorbera, Italy): 1M+ downloads, 4.1–4.18★ on ~52K–53.9K reviews.** Free with ads; no in-app purchases on the free listing itself, but a separate paid tier "Big Days Pro" exists at **$1.99** (one-time purchase model, closer to the "remove ads" pattern under consideration for Sofrim Yamim). "Updated on" date differs materially by source: 2024-10-19 (aggregator) vs 2025-11-14 (direct fetch) — the direct fetch is the more current/authoritative read. [Source: Google Play via r.jina.ai, accessed 2026-08-17, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.astrovicapp.bigDaysLite&hl=en_US; corroborating aggregator (AppBrain-derived) search snippet, accessed 2026-08-17]. Confidence: high on scale/rating/price, medium on exact update-recency given the source disagreement. Class: competitor.

- **Astrovic App is a small solo/indie developer, not a funded company**: 7 apps on Google Play, active since 2015, ~4M total installs and ~60K total ratings across the whole portfolio (biggest being Big Days and My Big Days). This is evidence against "funded/scaling competitor" risk for at least one major player in the category. [Source: AppBrain developer profile via Google Search snippet, accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Astrovic+App/; corroborating: astrovicapps.com support/contact info surfaced in same search]. Confidence: low — **unverified single-source** (only one aggregator surfaced this data; a second independent read was not obtained this round; "millions of users" is marketing language and should be discounted). - **No dedicated Israeli/Hebrew "countdown to Jewish holiday" native app was found; the closest identified substitute is a free website, not an app**: yo-yoo.co.il offers a Hebrew-language "days remaining" calculator explicitly covering "days remaining until Jewish holidays," positioned as a simple web calculator/tool rather than an installable app. This is a concrete, named substitute that serves the exact "how many days until [holiday]" need at zero cost and zero install friction. [Source: yo-yoo.co.il, "עוד כמה ימים עד לתאריך", accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.yo-yoo.co.il/tools/days-remined/]. Confidence: high (directly fetched and read the page content). Class: substitute.

- **Google Calendar's built-in Hebrew calendar overlay (Settings > Alternative Calendar > Hebrew) is a pre-installed, zero-cost substitute** that syncs across Android and surfaces Hebrew dates/holidays without requiring a new app install — reducing the addressable audience for any paid or ad-supported dedicated holiday app. [Source: Google Search result summarizing Google Calendar help documentation, accessed 2026-08-17]. Confidence: low (not independently re-verified by direct fetch of a Google support page this round — carried over from round 1, still unconfirmed by a primary Google source). Class: substitute.

- **Appvestor ApS (the developer entity behind the 10M+-install "Countdown Days App & Widget") traces to a Denmark-based company (founded 2017) that pivoted from an app-funding platform and was reportedly acquired by Calldorado on 2021-09-08; it now describes itself as a "data- and AI-driven growth engine for app publishers."** This is the one piece of evidence in this research of M&A/scaling activity touching a competitor in the category — though it is evidence about the publisher/growth-platform entity, not confirmation that Appvestor directly built or still actively develops the countdown app itself. [Source: Tracxn company profile via Google Search snippet, accessed 2026-08-17, https://tracxn.com/d/companies/appvestor/]. Confidence: low — **unverified single-source**, and the link between "Appvestor ApS" the growth-platform company and "Glad Appvestor" the Play Store developer name on the countdown app was not independently confirmed by reading a primary Appvestor or Play Store developer page. Class: other (funding/trajectory), flagged for follow-up.

## Leads

- The install-count leaderboard among generic (non-Hebrew) countdown apps found this round, largest to smallest: Countdown Days App & Widget (10M+, but weakest rating ~3.3★) > Time Until (5M+, ~4.6★) > Big Days (1M+, ~4.1★) > Countdown to Anything (500K+, ~4.5★). Pattern worth another dimension's attention: the highest-install app has the worst rating and a reviewed trust/permissions controversy — suggests install count and user satisfaction are not correlated in this category, relevant to positioning strategy.
- Big Days Pro's $1.99 one-time price point is the only confirmed concrete "remove ads"-style price found directly on a competitor listing — useful anchor for Sofrim Yamim's own IAP pricing decision (a pricing/monetization-focused research dimension may want to gather more such price points, e.g. from Countdown to Anything and Time Until once their exact subscription/lifetime prices are visible, which were not disclosed on the listing text alone and may need a locale/region-specific price check).
- The Appvestor ApS ↔ Calldorado acquisition lead is worth a dedicated follow-up (business/funding-research dimension) to confirm whether this affects the "Countdown Days App & Widget" product roadmap specifically.
- Whether com.kiki.hebrewcalendar's apparent delisting is permanent, and why, is worth a quick follow-up (e.g., checking the developer's other listings or a Hebrew tech-news mention) if the Hebrew-market dimension needs more certainty.
- No evidence was gathered on WhatsApp group holiday reminders or physical planners/calendars as substitutes specifically for the Israeli market — this remains open and may need a dedicated qualitative/user-research pass rather than a web-search pass, since this kind of informal behavior is unlikely to be documented in indexed web sources.
