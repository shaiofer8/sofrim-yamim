# Market Size & Growth — Round 1

Broad mapping queries: Israel smartphone/Android penetration, calendar-app category size, countdown-app download/revenue estimates, Hebrew-market app landscape.

## Findings

- **Israel total population was 9.55 million as of October 2025, growing +1.4% (+130,000) year-over-year.** [Source: DataReportal, "Digital 2026: Israel", 2026, accessed 2026-08-17, https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-israel]. Confidence: high. Class: market-size.
- **Israel had 8.72 million internet users as of October 2025, a 91.3% penetration rate, up +144,000 (+1.7%) year-over-year; 829,000 people (8.7%) remained offline.** [Source: DataReportal, "Digital 2026: Israel", 2026, accessed 2026-08-17, https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-israel]. Confidence: high. Class: market-size.
- **Israel had 10.4 million active cellular mobile connections in late 2025, equivalent to 109% of total population (multi-SIM effects); 99.7% of these are broadband-capable. This figure counts SIM connections, not unique smartphone users, so it overstates the smartphone user base.** [Source: DataReportal, "Digital 2026: Israel", 2026, accessed 2026-08-17, https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-israel]. Confidence: high. Class: market-size.
- **No Android-vs-iOS OS split or dedicated smartphone-user-count figure was present in the DataReportal Digital 2026 Israel report itself** — flagged under Could Not Find below; pursued separately via Statcounter (see Round 2).
- **The global calendar-app market was valued at roughly $2.5 billion in 2024, projected to reach $5.4 billion by 2033 at a 9.4% CAGR (2026–2033).** [Source: DataHorizzon Research, "Calendar App Market Size, Growth, Share, & Analysis Report", 2026 (report page, no clear primary publication date), accessed 2026-08-17, https://datahorizzonresearch.com/calendar-app-market-46806]. Confidence: low. Class: market-size. (Paid-research-firm boilerplate; conflicts with a second source below — see Leads.)
- **A competing paid-research estimate puts the calendar-app market at $2.35 billion by 2035 at a 7.8% CAGR (2026–2035) — a different total-market trajectory from the DataHorizzon figure above despite covering the same category.** [Source: WiseGuy Reports, "Calendar App Market Trends & Growth Analysis 2035", 2026, accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/calendar-app-market]. Confidence: low. Class: market-size. Contradicts the DataHorizzon figure above (different base year, different CAGR, no shared methodology disclosed) — do not average; treat category-market-size-in-dollars as unresolved/low-value for this decision.
- **Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, and Apple Calendar together control roughly 67% of global calendar-app users**, per one paid-research summary — cited without primary sourcing. [Source: 6sense / summarized market report language surfaced via WiseGuy/DataHorizzon search results, 2026, accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/calendar-app-market]. Confidence: low. Class: market-size.
- **Sensor Tower's State of Mobile 2026 report: global app downloads (iOS + Google Play) reached nearly 150 billion in 2025, up 0.8% year-over-year; global in-app-purchase (IAP) revenue reached $167 billion, up 10.6% year-over-year — both described as all-time highs.** [Source: Sensor Tower, "2026 State of Mobile", 2026-02 (per investgame.net PDF footer date), accessed 2026-08-17, https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-2026]. Confidence: medium (figures are Sensor Tower's own modeled estimates, a reputable primary-ish source for this industry, but I have not yet fetched the full report PDF to confirm exact wording). Class: market-size.
- **A generic "Countdown" app tracked by Sensor Tower earned an estimated $30,000 in monthly revenue and 500,000 downloads in July 2024** (app identity/publisher not disambiguated in the search snippet; likely one of the "Countdown" App Store listings, not necessarily Google Play). [Source: Sensor Tower app overview page (App Store, US), "Countdown", dated as of ~2024-07, accessed 2026-08-17, https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1403367428?country=US]. Confidence: low. Class: market-size. Stale relative to the ≤18-month freshness bar for market-size claims (data point is from mid-2024, now >2 years old) — flag as historical, not current.
- **"Days Matter" (com.clover.daysmatter), a countdown/event-tracking app on Google Play, has been live since August 2015 and is categorized as a Productivity app.** [Source: Similarweb app statistics page for com.clover.daysmatter, accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.similarweb.com/app/google-play/com.clover.daysmatter/statistics] — note: a search-engine summary of this same page separately claimed "~20,000 downloads in the last 30 days, 2 million total downloads," but a direct fetch of the live page did not surface those figures (they were paywalled). Treat the specific download numbers as **unverified/unconfirmed** — see Round 2 for the direct-fetch result and Could Not Find.
- **"הלוח העברי" (HaLuach HaIvri / Hebrew Calendar), an Israeli Hebrew-calendar/holiday-times app by iEnd Software Development Ltd (founded 2008), advertises 3,000,000+ downloads and hundreds of thousands of active users per month, with a 4.7-star rating from ~30,000 reviews.** [Source: hebcal.co.il (developer's own site), accessed 2026-08-17, https://hebcal.co.il/?lang=en]. Confidence: medium (primary source — the developer's own reported figure — but self-reported, undated, and not independently corroborated by a second source; does not meet the two-source rule for a confirmed market-size figure). Class: market-size.

## Leads

- **הלוח העברי (iEnd Software Development Ltd)** is the standout Israel-specific lead: a Hebrew calendar/Jewish-holiday app with a claimed 3M+ downloads — the closest thing to an incumbent in the "Hebrew holiday-aware calendar/lifestyle app" niche that Sofrim Yamim would compete adjacent to. Worth a dedicated competitive-dimension look (pricing/ads model, review sentiment, feature set, whether it does countdowns).
- **HebDate** and **לוח שנה עברי (com.kobisnir.hebrewcalendar)** are other Israeli Hebrew-calendar apps surfaced in search — not yet sized.
- Contradiction flagged: the two paid "calendar app market" size reports (DataHorizzon vs WiseGuy) disagree materially on both dollar figure and CAGR for what claims to be the same category — signals these are low-rigor SEO/lead-gen market reports, not reliable inputs for a market-size claim.
- Country-level (Israel-specific) download/revenue data for niche countdown/calendar apps appears to sit behind Similarweb/Sensor Tower paywalls — free-tier access only surfaces global/US rankings, not Israel-specific figures.
- Worth checking in a later round: Appfigures public blog posts, and Sensor Tower's full State-of-Mobile-2026 PDF (not just search snippets) for a Lifestyle/Utilities category-level download or revenue breakdown.
