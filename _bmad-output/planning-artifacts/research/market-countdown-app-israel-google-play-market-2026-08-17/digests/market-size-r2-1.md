# Market Size & Growth — Round 2

Targeted follow-ups from Round 1: confirm Android/iOS split for Israel, verify the Days Matter download figures via direct fetch, check Sensor Tower category-level trends, and try to source-verify the הלוח העברי download claim.

## Findings

- **In Israel, Android holds 67.56% of mobile OS market share vs. iOS at 32.44%, as of July 2026 (most recent month shown).** [Source: Statcounter Global Stats, "Mobile Operating System Market Share Israel", data through 2026-07, accessed 2026-08-17, https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/israel]. Confidence: medium (Statcounter measures share of web page-view traffic by OS, a widely used industry proxy for device/OS install base, not a direct census of unique users — single source only, no second measurement service cross-checked this round). Class: market-size.
- **Direct fetch of the live Similarweb page for "Days Matter – Countdown Event" (com.clover.daysmatter) does NOT show the "~20,000 downloads/30 days, 2 million total downloads" figures that a search-engine summary attributed to this same URL in Round 1 — those numbers are paywalled/locked on the actual page (marked "Unlock daily active users", no download counts shown free).** [Source: Similarweb, "Days Matter - Countdown Event App Stats", data as of 2026-08-14, accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.similarweb.com/app/google-play/com.clover.daysmatter/statistics/]. Confidence: n/a (this is a correction/retraction of a Round-1 claim, not a new positive claim). Class: other. **The earlier "2 million total downloads" figure should be treated as unconfirmed and not cited as fact** — likely a search-summarizer artifact from a stale cache or a different data view.
- **Days Matter (com.clover.daysmatter) ranks #2,056 in the Productivity category on Google Play in the US by Similarweb's usage-rank algorithm (installs + active users over a trailing 28-day window), and ranks much higher in Taiwan (#37), Malaysia (#188), Japan (#444), Spain (#511), and Poland (#665) — Israel does not appear among its listed top markets.** [Source: Similarweb, "Days Matter - Countdown Event App Stats", data as of 2026-08-14, accessed 2026-08-17, https://www.similarweb.com/app/google-play/com.clover.daysmatter/statistics/]. Confidence: medium (single source; ranking methodology is Similarweb's own algorithm, not raw download counts). Class: market-size.
- **Per Sensor Tower's State of Mobile 2026 report, the Lifestyle app category experienced one of the steepest download declines of any category in 2025, and in the Lifestyle & Puzzle segments, marketing ad-spend share exceeds IAP-revenue share in almost every market — e.g., US advertisers directed 56% of ad spend into Lifestyle & Puzzle, a share much larger than that segment's share of IAP revenue.** [Source: Sensor Tower, "2026 State of Mobile" (as summarized in secondary coverage), 2026-02, accessed 2026-08-17, https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-2026]. Confidence: medium (Sensor Tower is the primary modeler, but this was read via a search-result summary rather than the full report; not yet independently corroborated by a second source). Class: behavior. Relevant to Sofrim Yamim's ad-supported model: Lifestyle-category apps are systematically over-monetized via ads relative to what users pay in, i.e., ad revenue is the norm and IAP-only conversion is comparatively weak in this category — supports a banner-ads-plus-remove-ads-IAP model over a pure-IAP one, though this is a global (not Israel) data point.
- **The הלוח העברי "3,000,000+ downloads" and "4.7 stars / ~30,000 ratings" claim (from Round 1) is confirmed to originate directly from the developer's own promotional site, hebcal.co.il, with no independent second source found this round and no publication date attached to the figure.** [Source: hebcal.co.il, developer site, accessed 2026-08-17, https://hebcal.co.il/?lang=en]. Confidence: medium (primary/self-reported; does not meet the two-source rule). Class: market-size. **Flag: unverified single-source** per the two-source rule — treat as a plausible upper-bound claim by the developer, not a confirmed market fact.
- **Attempts to fetch Statista's Israel smartphone-penetration page and AppBrain's Days Matter page both failed this round** (Statista: redirect loop / paywall; AppBrain: HTTP 403) — neither could be used to corroborate or add to smartphone-penetration or download-count figures. Class: other.

## Derived estimate (not a sourced claim — shown for transparency, built from two Round-1/Round-2 sourced figures)

- Combining DataReportal's **8.72 million internet users in Israel (Oct 2025)** with Statcounter's **67.56% Android share of Israeli mobile OS traffic (Jul 2026)** yields an arithmetic outer-bound estimate of **~5.9 million Android-using internet users in Israel**. This is a back-of-envelope combination of two different measurement methodologies (a population survey-based internet-user count and a web-traffic-based OS-share metric), not a single reported figure — it should be treated as a rough ceiling on the Android-reachable population, before any further narrowing for Hebrew-speaking, holiday/event-interested, Google-Play-active users specifically. No source directly reports an Israel Android *user count*; this figure is not independently confirmed and should not be quoted as a standalone market-size fact.

## Leads

- The Round-1 "2 million downloads" Days Matter figure appears to have been a search-summarizer artifact not backed by the actual page content — a caution for how much to trust WebSearch's own prose summaries versus the underlying fetched page in later rounds/dimensions.
- Sensor Tower's Lifestyle-category ad-spend-vs-IAP-revenue imbalance (US: 56% ad spend share) is a potentially load-bearing data point for the pricing/positioning decision (banner ads + one-time remove-ads IAP) — worth a dedicated fetch of the full State of Mobile 2026 PDF in a pricing-focused research dimension, since this round only saw it via secondary summary.
- No Israel-specific or Hebrew-market Sensor Tower/Similarweb/Appfigures category report was found in either round — this looks like a genuine data gap (Israel is a small enough Play Store market that neither major analytics vendor appears to publish free country-level category reports for it) rather than a search-strategy failure — two rounds of both English and Hebrew queries surfaced no such report.
